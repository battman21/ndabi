Repair two harness defects without changing the binary operation or aggregate performance gates.

## 2. Frozen execution and gates

Set a process case to passed after its exact-result and request-compression gates pass.
Apply the native-distance, speed, and allocation gates only to the five-process median.
Require every process report to pass its process gates.

Remove the exact temporary container after the campaign.
Retry removal three times.
Fail the campaign when the exact container still exists.

## 3. Attribution and boundaries

Attribute the large gain to a fused operation and one engine round trip.
Attribute request compression to the non-human-readable frame.
Do not claim that binary decoding alone makes MongoDB faster.

This campaign proves one bounded lossless read operation through local Docker.
It does not prove all MongoDB functions, writes, transactions, sharding, Atlas, WAN behaviour, model tokens, energy, or production custody.
